Turn your photos into paint-by-numbers projects to print and paint on paper.

Create a project

Select a photo, choose 8 or 16 colors, and compare the numbered drawing with the color preview. All conversion happens on your iPhone.

Export and print

Choose A4 or US Letter, then Export PDF. Save to Files, share, or print from the system share sheet. The PDF has a drawing page and a color guide. Print at 100% or Actual Size.

Get a clearer drawing

Start with a well-lit photo with a clear subject. Choose 8 colors for a simpler project. Fine details may be lost; narrow regions may not have enough room for a number.

Troubleshooting

If an import fails, try another photo. For an export issue, try again and check available storage. Requires iOS 16 or later. For help, email us with your iPhone model, iOS version and a description of the issue. You do not need to send your photo.
